## Plan  *(leader writes; Gate 1 approves)*

- **Goal:** 3E complete: ASCII, streaming (byte-at-a-time and coalesced
  frames), `reset()`, the 4E vectors kept for v2, and the codec's
  zero-allocation proof.
- **Approach:** 3E ASCII through the same QnA command code (ASCII field
  codec); A.3/A.4 (4E) transcribed and tagged `v2`, skipped by the runner;
  `test_alloc.cpp` for `mc_core_protocol_tests` reuses
  `tests/common/alloc_counter.h`.
- **Files expected to change:** `src/core/protocol/frame_3e.cpp` (ASCII),
  `tests/vectors/3e_ascii.vec`, `tests/vectors/4e_binary.vec`,
  `tests/vectors/4e_ascii.vec`, `tests/core/protocol/test_frame_3e.cpp`,
  `tests/core/protocol/test_parser_stream.cpp`,
  `tests/core/protocol/test_alloc.cpp`, `tests/CMakeLists.txt`.
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  1. Every A.2 vector round-trips; 3E-01…13 pass for ASCII; A.3/A.4 exist,
     tagged `v2`, and are skipped (the runner reports them as skipped).
  2. STR-01 (every 3E response fed one byte at a time), STR-02 (two 3E frames
     in one buffer; `frameLength()` points at the second) and STR-04 (cut
     short, `reset()`, recovers) pass.
  3. ALC-01 for the codec: zero allocations for encode-into-buffer,
     byte-at-a-time `feed` and `payload()` on 3E Binary and ASCII.
  4. `ctest -L "core_protocol|core_model|build"` green on MSVC.
