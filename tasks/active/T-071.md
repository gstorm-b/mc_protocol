## Plan  *(leader writes; Gate 1 approves)*

- **Goal:** the "Frame trace", "Debug log" and "Capture and export" rows of the spec.
- **Approach:** per tab, a trace view (tx / rx chunks, nanosecond stamps, hex and ASCII, frame
  boundaries, optional decode) fed by the runner's batched copies; a log view of every `LogSink`
  (session, device, mock) with level / tab filters, search, copy, save; bounded ring buffers
  (configurable) everywhere; capture of a tab's traffic with `CaptureWriter` in the `hil-capture`
  formats. Export of a real-PLC capture as replay test data to `tests/vectors/captured/<profile-id>/`
  (the owner confirms before `git add`); captures of mocks or `virtual_plc` only to a user folder,
  never to `tests/vectors/captured/`.
- **Acceptance criteria:** GUI-05 passes (memory bounded and GUI event latency below a stated
  limit while a mock answers as fast as it can for N seconds); a capture made in the GUI against a
  mock replays green with `mc_replay_tests --replay-root=...`; the export refuses a mock or
  `virtual_plc` capture as a target under `tests/vectors/captured/`.
