## Plan  *(leader writes; Gate 1 approves)*

- **Goal:** the threading backbone of the spec's "Architecture": every device and mock on its own
  runner thread, the GUI thread never blocked, failures contained, clean shutdown.
- **Approach:** in `mc_workbench_core` (no widgets):
  - `RunnerThread` owning one `QThread`; the runner object is **created inside** the thread
    (`QThread::started`), so `McDevice` / `MockPlc` and their children are born there.
  - `DeviceRunner`: owns `McDevice` (+ the `RecordingTransport` of `mc_hil_tool`); commands via
    queued `invokeMethod` (connect, disconnect, setConfig, subscribe, unsubscribe, submit); results
    and events as queued signals carrying **value copies**; batching of values/frames/log lines at
    ≤ ~30 emits per second.
  - `MockRunner` (basic): `MockPlc` served over loopback TCP — its own serving code
    (`examples/virtual_plc` stays unchanged, owner decision); COM serving comes in T-070.
  - Containment: every runner slot catches exceptions, reports a tab error, stops that runner.
  - Shutdown: stop → bounded wait → report a stuck thread instead of blocking; objects deleted on
    their own thread.
- **Acceptance criteria:** GUI-01, GUI-03, GUI-04 of the spec pass on MSVC and MinGW; a test asserts
  `QObject::thread()` of the device and the mock is the runner thread, never the GUI thread.
- **Gate 1 approval:** 2026-10-03 — leader (Phase 8 delegation). Common rules: `T-067.md`.
