## Leader decisions on the blocking question (2026-10-01)

Both are defects of earlier phases that the 3E-only tests could not reach; the fixes stay
inside the specs (no spec or public-header change), so they are authorised under the Phase 6
delegation and done in this task, listed under Deviations, reviewed at T-044.

1. `chunkCount()`/`chunk()` must not apply `validate()` rule 6 (field maximum) to the
   **unsplit** request: `chunk()` exists to split by `maxPoints()` (SPEC-core-model `limits.h`,
   spec §8.5); rule 6 stays on the encode path (1E-14). Implement it cleanly (an internal
   validation without rule 6 for the chunking path, writes handled with rule 7 intact), not by
   clamping a copy. New CHK test(s): a 1E read above 256 units chunks to the §8.5 count; a 1E
   write above 256 with/without `splitWrites`; encode of 257 points still `PointCount`.
2. `Session::subscribe()`'s immediate frame check must check what the plan will read: the
   word-aligned range (share `alignToWordBoundary` with `read_plan.cpp`, incl. the M9000
   origin on 1E/1C), not the raw subscription. New SES test: `subscribe(M8180, 10)` on 1E
   with `bitsAsWords` on succeeds; a truly invalid head still fails at once.
3. `Corruption::WrongRoute` on 1E leaves the response unchanged (a 1E response has no route/PC
   field — the Plan was wrong): accepted. Other open behaviours (failRange low byte, PC not
   checked, unsupported request data → `unsupported1e`, no per-command point limits): accepted.
