## Plan  *(leader writes; Gate 1 approves)*

- **Goal:** clients of one mock never share a parser: interleaved partial requests of two clients
  and a request abandoned by a closed client no longer corrupt anything. The full
  `tests/hil/e2e/plan_3e.json`, E-10 included, runs against a GUI mock tab.
- **Approach:**
  1. `include/mc/mock/mock_plc.h` + `src/mock/`: the API of `SPEC-mock-plc.md` "Public API"
     (amended 2026-10-04): `MockStreamId`, `openStream()`, `closeStream()`, `bytesIn(id, …)`,
     `nextResponse(id, …)`, `MockRequestRecord::stream`; the stream-less calls are stream 0.
     Reception state (partial request, serial scan, F2 block state) and the response queue move
     per stream; memory, faults, log and counters stay shared ("Streams" section). Independence
     rule (MCK-HYG) unchanged.
  2. Tests MCK-13 in `mc_mock_tests` on every frame family; MCK-01…12, INT-* unchanged and green;
     mock coverage bar 90 kept.
  3. `tools/mc_workbench/mock_runner.*`: one stream per TCP client (opened on connect, closed on
     disconnect); COM serving uses stream 0. The T-070 "shared parser" known limit is removed
     from the docs.
  4. GUI tests: two TCP clients sending interleaved fragments to one mock tab both get correct
     answers; the full `plan_3e.json` (E-10 included) runs against a GUI mock tab and replays
     green; T-072's reduced plan test may stay or be widened.
  5. `CHANGELOG.md` (Unreleased): the new `MockPlc` stream API.
- **Acceptance criteria:** MCK-13 green on MSVC and MinGW; all labels green on both (35+/35+);
  `check.ps1` both kits + `check.sh`; mock coverage ≥ 90; the two GUI tests above green; no
  change to `examples/virtual_plc`, the HIL gate or `tools/hil_capture`.
- **Gate 1 approval:** 2026-10-04 — leader, under the owner's delegation for the Phase 8 follow-ups (`rules.md`); spec text approved by the owner the same day. Gate 2: leader, one commit for T-075…T-077 after tester and reviewer pass.
