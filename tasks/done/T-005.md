- Findings (severity · file:line · what/why):
  1. **should-fix · `scripts/check.ps1:197`** — `$ctestDryRun = & ctest --test-dir $dir -N 2>&1`

     script-wide (line 43). In PowerShell 5.1, a native command's stderr line captured this way
     becomes a non-terminating `NativeCommandError`; under `'Stop'` that gets promoted to a
     **terminating** exception, which would abort stage 4 with an unhandled PowerShell error
